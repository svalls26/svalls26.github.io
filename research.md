  <h1 id="research-heading" class="bio__eyebrow">Research</h1>
  <p>My research interests centre on human-centred machine learning: how AI can support complex, context dependent tasks, how human expertise can improve everyday workflows, and how models can be adapted through interaction data.</p>
  <p>I am interested in bringing AI closer to expert domains, improving decision making and creating meaningful impact across history, science and society.</p>
  <h2>Human–AI collaboration for manuscript reconstruction</h2>
  <p>For my MPhil dissertation in Machine Learning and Machine Intelligence at the University of Cambridge, I created a human–AI collaboration system to help reconstruct manuscripts from the <a href="https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/discarded-history-treasures-of-the-cairo-genizah">Cairo Genizah</a>.</p>
  <p>Developed through a participatory design approach with scholars, the system uses a computer vision pipeline to retrieve potential joins based on visual similarity. The project was selected for the <a href="https://www.ai.cam.ac.uk/news/from-safer-workplaces-to-medieval-manuscripts-five-new-ai-deas-projects-put-ai-to-work-on-real-world-problems/">ai@cam AI-deas Sprint</a> to develop it into a production-ready system for practical use.</p>
